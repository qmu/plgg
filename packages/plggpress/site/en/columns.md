# Reading in columns

Open [Markdown and links](/markdown/). This page stays on the left and the article opens on its right. A link in the article opens a third column. To follow another line of inquiry, choose a different link in an earlier column. It replaces the columns to its right.

Each added column has a close button in its header. Closing a column also closes those to its right. To bring a particular column into view, choose its title in the list of open columns.

## Share what you’re reading

Copy the address from the browser. The path identifies the first page, and the `c` query parameter lists the pages to its right in order. For example, `/en/columns/?c=/en/markdown/,/en/sheets/` describes three open pages. The Japanese edition uses the same paths without `/en`. Reloading or using the browser’s Back button restores the corresponding columns.

## On a narrow screen

Scroll sideways to bring an earlier column back into view. Scroll vertically to read within a column. Use the menu button at the top to open the sidebar. To let readers consult another site in the same strip, see [Joining sites](/joining/).
