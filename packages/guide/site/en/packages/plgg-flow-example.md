# plgg-flow-example — Exercise diagrams from manuscripts

Compare diagram manuscripts with their rendered exhibits. The drawing component lives in plgg-flow and checks on diagram references and types live in plgg-ir-diagram. [Open the diagram exhibits](https://plgg-flow.qmu.co.jp/).
